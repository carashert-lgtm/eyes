### FAQ — Launch Support Window

**What is the launch support window?**  
A **14-day period** before public launch where community members can optionally contribute to a **pre-launch growth pool** used **100%** for launch visibility and distribution.

**Is this an investment?**  
**No.** It is voluntary support for launch operations (content, community, visibility). We do not promise financial returns, price movement, or personal gain. This is not financial advice.

**Where does the money go?**  
**100%** toward launch growth efforts: content distribution, community growth, launch visibility, creator/partner outreach, and campaign execution — as described before the window opens.

**Does this change tokenomics?**  
**No.** On-chain trading fees remain **1%** with a **50% creator / 50% buy & burn $EYES** split. The growth pool is a separate pre-launch operational mechanism.

**What happens after 14 days?**  
The window **closes**, contributions stop, and the team publishes a **factual allocation recap**. Marketing shifts to the **public launch** phase.

**Can I expect the token to go up because of this?**  
We **do not** promise outcomes or price movement. Contributions support **launch readiness and visibility** only.

**How do I participate?**  
Follow official links only (`/launch-support` and pinned TG/X posts). Ignore DMs with “special deals.”

## FAQ — common objections

### “Is this just another launchpad?”
No — the difference is **enforced opening rules** (Eyes Window), **permanent LP lock in a no-withdraw locker**, and a **platform token flywheel** where **50% of every launch fee buys and burns $EYES**. Most pads market fairness; we encode it.

### “Can snipers still win?”
Snipers optimize for unprotected T0. Eyes Window blocks wallet-to-wallet and sell paths during the opening; only approved buy sources (pair/router) can distribute tokens. After the window, trading is normal. We don’t claim zero MEV — we remove the usual **retail-last** opening.

### “How do I know LP is really locked?”
LP tokens go to `EyesLiquidityLocker` — **no withdraw function**. Verify the LP balance on-chain and the lock event from the factory. Don’t trust a screenshot.

### “Where does the 1% fee go?”
**50%** to the launch creator in ETH, instantly. **50%** to the buy-and-burn queue — swapped into $EYES and burned by the keeper executor. Official model is **50/50**. See [`TOKENOMICS.md`](TOKENOMICS.md).

### “Is there a team allocation / insider bag?”
**Not in the official model.** $EYES is **1B fixed supply**, minted once at deploy. If that ever changes, it must be announced on-chain and updated in public docs first.

### “Does every launch token get burned?”
**No.** Launch tokens are not burned by default. The burn mechanic targets **$EYES** — linking every launch’s volume to platform-token deflation.

### “Wen Launch App / frontend?”
Marketing site is live. Full swap + launch UI comes **after** testnet soak and audit. Until then, mechanics are provable via scripts and testnet — not a polished app.

### “Wen mainnet?”
After **testnet soak**, **contract verification**, and **external audit** — not before. Follow announcements for a specific date; ignore rumor channels.

### “Is $EYES an investment?”
We provide **infrastructure documentation**, not financial advice. $EYES captures platform activity through a **buy-and-burn loop**. Do your own research. Never risk what you can’t lose.

### “Why Base?”
Base fits low-cost deployment and Uniswap V2-style liquidity for fair launches. Chain choice is operational — the mechanics are EVM-portable in principle.

### “What if the keeper stops?”
Burns queue until the keeper runs `executeBuyAndBurn`. Operators should schedule keeper runs (`-Step keeper`). Queued ETH stays in the collector until executed — monitor `accumulatedBurnProceeds`.

### “Can creators customize fees?”
Factory accepts per-launch BPS config; **defaults are 50/50** when unset. Public story stays 50/50 unless you explicitly announce a different curated launch deal.
