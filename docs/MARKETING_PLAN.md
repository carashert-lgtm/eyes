# Eyes Open ($EYES) — Marketing Plan

**Version:** 1.0 · **Audience:** solo operator or 2–3 person team  
**Slogan:** Eyes Open. No Snipers. No Games.

This plan assumes you are pre-mainnet or early testnet-public. Do not market mainnet readiness until contracts are deployed, verified, and soaked on testnet.

---

## 1. Positioning

### What we are
**Eyes Open** is a fair-launch pad on Base where every project gets enforced rules — not promises: **Eyes Window** (gated opening), **100% permanent LP lock**, and a **1% trading fee** split **50% creator / 50% buy & burn $EYES**.

### What we are not
- Not a meme factory
- Not a “guaranteed 100x” launchpad
- Not a bot-friendly sniper playground
- Not a fully shipped consumer app yet (launch app / swap UI comes after testnet proof)

### Positioning statement
> For crypto builders and serious early buyers who are tired of rigged T0 launches, **Eyes Open** is the fair-launch infrastructure that puts **real users first** and routes platform volume into a **transparent $EYES buy-and-burn flywheel** — because **fair mechanics should be on-chain, not in a thread.**

### Competitive angle (honest)
| Them | Us |
|------|-----|
| “Fair launch” as marketing | Eyes Window + transfer rules in contract |
| LP lock “trust me” | LP sent to locker with no withdraw |
| Platform token disconnected from volume | 50% of every launch fee buys & burns $EYES |
| Snipers win block zero | Gated window blocks P2P/sells during opening |

---

## 2. Target audience

### Primary — Launch creators (builders)
- Solo devs, small teams, community-led projects on Base
- Already burned by bot-dominated launches
- Want credibility: locked LP + clear fee split + on-chain proof

**Message:** “Launch fair without rebuilding the stack.”

### Secondary — Early buyers (informed retail)
- Traders who care about *how* a token opened, not just ticker hype
- Allergic to bundler/sniper meta
- Will read tokenomics before aping

**Message:** “You’re not block zero bait.”

### Tertiary — Base / fair-launch community operators
- Mods, alpha group admins, devrel-friendly accounts
- Potential partners for first curated launches (not paid shills)

**Message:** “Infrastructure you can link to, not a black box.”

### Who to deprioritize (for now)
- Pure degen flipper accounts looking for 5-minute pumps
- Bot operators (they are the antagonist, not the customer)
- Audiences expecting a polished mobile app on day one

---

## 3. Core narrative

Use this arc in every channel:

1. **Problem:** Normal launches are rigged before humans arrive — snipers, bundlers, multi-wallet games.
2. **Mechanism:** Eyes Open enforces an **Eyes Window** — only approved buy paths during opening; LP **locked forever**.
3. **Platform token:** Every launch trade pays **1%** → **50% creator / 50% buy & burn $EYES**.
4. **Proof:** Link testnet txs, contract addresses, docs — not vibes.
5. **CTA:** Read tokenomics → follow build → join community → (later) launch or trade on-platform.

**Never lead with price.** Lead with mechanics and proof.

---

## 4. Content pillars

Rotate content across these five pillars. Each post should map to exactly one.

| Pillar | Purpose | Example topics |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| **A. The rigged launch problem** | Awareness, shared enemy | Snipers, bundlers, fake “fair” launches |
| **B. Eyes Window explained** | Education | What’s blocked, what’s allowed, why it matters |
| **C. On-chain proof** | Credibility | LP lock, fee router, burn txs, testnet demo |
| **D. $EYES flywheel** | Token narrative | 50/50 split, buy/burn loop, fixed 1B supply |
| **E. Builder / operator updates** | Trust & retention | Testnet progress, audit prep, docs, timelines |

**Ratio (weekly):** 30% problem/education · 30% mechanism · 20% proof · 10% tokenomics · 10% build updates

## 5. Channel strategy

### X (Twitter) — primary broadcast
**Role:** Discovery, narrative, proof links, founder voice.

**Tactics:**
- 1–2 posts/day in pre-launch; 3–5 on launch day
- Threads for Eyes Window + tokenomics (link `/tokenomics`)
- Quote-tweet Base ecosystem news only when you have a genuine tie-in
- Pin: problem → solution → link (see `MESSAGING_KIT.md`)

**Avoid:** Engagement bait, fake countdowns, “stealth launch” teasing with no substance.

### Telegram — community hub
**Role:** Announcements, FAQ, testnet links, support routing.

**Structure:**
- `#announcements` (admin only)
- `#general` (discussion)
- `#support` (questions → docs first)
- `#launches` (future: curated launch alerts)

**Tactics:**
- Post weekly recap (what shipped, what’s next)
- Pin: website + tokenomics + “no financial advice” disclaimer

**Avoid:** Price calls, “buy now” pressure, allowing scam links unp moderated.

### Discord (optional — add when TG exceeds ~500 active)
**Role:** Same as Telegram but better for async dev/builder chat.

**Start Discord only if** you can moderate daily. One quiet Discord hurts more than no Discord.

### Crypto communities (outbound)
**Where:** Base-focused TG groups, fair-launch Twitter circles, builder Discords (with permission).

**Tactics:**
- Share **one** educational thread + doc link — not drive-by shilling
- Offer **first curated testnet launch** to one serious builder as case study
- Ask for feedback on mechanics, not hype

**Avoid:** Spamming 20 groups with the same paste; paid KOL packages you can’t verify.

## 6. 14-day Launch Support Window (community bootstrap)

**Separate from trading-fee tokenomics.** This is a pre-launch **operational** phase — not an investment product, not a promise of financial outcome.

### What it is
A fixed **14-day launch support window** before public launch. During this period, community members may make a **community bootstrap contribution** to the **pre-launch growth pool**.

### What the pool funds (100%)
All contributions are allocated to **launch visibility and distribution**:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Content distribution | Threads, explainers, campaign assets |
| Community growth | TG/X setup, moderation, pins |
| Launch visibility | Outreach, coordinated announcement timing |
| Creator / partner outreach | Builder conversations, curated launch prep |
| Campaign execution | Launch-week content calendar delivery |

**Operator rule:** Publish a simple allocation intent (even a bullet list) before the window opens. After close, post a factual recap of how funds were used — no hype, no ROI framing.

### What it is NOT (messaging guardrails)
- ❌ Not an investment, presale, or securities offering
- ❌ Not tied to the 1% / 50-50 trading-fee model
- ❌ Not a promise of token price movement or personal gain
- ❌ Not “pay now, profit later” — use **support**, **contribute**, **bootstrap**, **growth pool**

### Timing in the overall calendar

```
Week -4 to -2     Build, testnet deploy, website live
Week -2           Final checklist + support window announced
Days -14 to -1    LAUNCH SUPPORT WINDOW OPEN
Day 0             Public launch milestone (platform / token — pick one)
Week +1           Retention + proof posts
```

### Channel tactics during the window
- **X:** 1 post every 2–3 days explaining pool purpose + link `/launch-support` (not daily asks)
- **Telegram:** Pin window dates, FAQ from `MESSAGING_KIT.md`, disclaimer
- **Website:** Landing section + dedicated page live before window opens

### Transition at window close (Day 0 prep)
1. Post “window closed” + total pool size (if sharing numbers)
2. Publish allocation recap (categories, not promises)
3. Shift pin to public launch mechanics + tokenomics
4. Pause contribution CTAs on site or mark “closed”

See [`LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`](LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md) §6 and [`MESSAGING_KIT.md`](MESSAGING_KIT.md) Launch Support copy.

## 8. Launch-day plan

Pick **one primary milestone** for “launch day” — do not conflate these:

| Milestone type | Example | Marketing frame |
|----------------|---------|-----------------|
| **Public testnet** | Contracts verified on Base Sepolia | “Try the mechanics” |

| **First curated launch** | Partner project via factory | “Case study #1” |
| **$EYES mainnet** | Post-audit only | “Platform token live” |

### Launch-day timeline (UTC-friendly)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| **T-24h** | Final checklist sign-off (`LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`) |
| **T-2h** | TG announcement: “Going live in 2h — testnet/mainnet: [X]” |
| **T-0** | Publish pin post + website link + contract links |
| **T+30m** | Thread walkthrough: create launch → window → swap → fee split |

### Launch-day content (minimum)
1. Pin post (from messaging kit)

## 9. Post-launch retention plan

### Week 1 — Prove it works
- Daily: one on-chain proof post (fee paid, burn executed, LP locked)
- Host 1 AMA in Telegram (text-only is fine; 45 min)
- Publish “Launch #1 post-mortem” if a curated launch ran

### Weeks 2–4 — Habit + credibility
- Weekly metrics thread (honest, small numbers OK)
- One builder-focused post: “How to launch on Eyes Open”
- Recruit **second** curated launch (quality over quantity)
- Document keeper schedule + burn totals in public gist or TG pin

### Month 2+ — Depth
- Monthly transparency report: launches, volume, cumulative burns
- Partner with one Base community for co-education (not paid promo)
- Ship only **real** product updates (app, indexer) — announce when usable

### Retention loops
| Loop | Trigger | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Proof loop** | Burn executes | Post tx + supply context |
| **Builder loop** | New launch | Creator spotlight + lock proof |
| **Education loop** | Repeated FAQ | Update MESSAGING_KIT + pin |
| **Trust loop** | Audit / multisig step | Plain-language post, no jargon wall |

## 10. What NOT to do

### Launch support window
- ❌ “Invest now” / “early access returns” / ROI language
- ❌ Mix support pool with 50/50 trading-fee tokenomics in one sentence
- ❌ Promise price impact from contributions
- ❌ Close window without a factual allocation recap

### Narrative
- ❌ Promise “no bots ever” — say “designed to remove sniper advantage at T0”
- ❌ Imply guaranteed returns from buy/burn
- ❌ Compare to specific projects with unfounded “we’re better” claims
- ❌ Hide testnet vs mainnet status

### Token / legal
- ❌ “Investment advice” or price targets
- ❌ Paid shills without disclosure
- ❌ Fake volume or manufactured “community” bots
- ✅ **10% owner allocation** disclosed in tokenomics — do not imply “no insider bag”

### Operations
- ❌ Launch mainnet before testnet soak + audit plan
- ❌ Announce Launch App before wallet flow works
- ❌ Run keeper silently — burns are marketing proof when they happen
- ❌ Argue in replies for hours — link docs, move on

### Brand
- ❌ Cartoon mascot energy, emoji spam, “LFG 🚀🚀🚀” as default tone
- ❌ Generic meme-coin aesthetic — stay **clean, premium, sharp**
- ❌ Rebrand slogan or 50/50 split without updating `TOKENOMICS.md` + site
