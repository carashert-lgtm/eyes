## Before presale “LIVE” marketing

1. Set entity name and contact emails in Vercel (`NEXT_PUBLIC_LEGAL_*` in `.env.production.example`).
2. Have a qualified attorney review all pages for your jurisdiction (US, EU, UK, etc.).
3. Confirm presale checkbox on `/presale` is live after deploy.
4. Pin Risk Disclosure link in Discord/TG.

## What these documents cover

- No investment / securities advice; crypto risk warnings
- Presale mechanics (ETH to treasury, off-chain registration, discretionary delivery)
- Self-custody, wallet linking, Discord bot
- Privacy (Discord ID, wallets, tx hashes, Vercel/Railway logs)
- Cookies (Team Space session, wallet connect)
- AI use in content, code, and support
- Limitation of liability, arbitration, Delaware governing law (configurable)

**These templates do not guarantee you will not be sued.** They reduce ambiguity and show good-faith disclosure. Counsel review is still required for regulated markets or large raises.
